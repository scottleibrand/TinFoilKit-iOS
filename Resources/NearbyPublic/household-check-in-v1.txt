Household check-in worksheet

Write down the people you expect to check in with, an out-of-area contact, and your next check-in time. Agree on a time zone so everyone reads the time the same way.

Send a short update when you have a connection. Include your name, the date and time, your current location or landmark, what you need, and when you plan to check in again. Share exact locations only with people who need them.

Example: Sam | Tue 2:10 p.m. local | near the library | safe | next check-in at 4 p.m.

Keep a paper copy of contact details and meeting points. If you change plans, tell the group. Before setting out, check whether the route and meeting point are safe and accessible.
