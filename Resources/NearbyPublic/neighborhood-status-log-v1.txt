Neighborhood status log

Use this blank form to track public conditions such as a library reopening, a closed road, or posted utility work. Use a public landmark or a general area. Keep names, home addresses, phone numbers, access codes, and personal details out of the log.

Record when the condition was observed and when the report was made. Include the time zone. Mark firsthand observations as Observed and information verified with an official source as Confirmed. Mark other reports Unverified until you can check them.

Set a time to check each entry again or let it expire. If a condition changes or an entry was wrong, add a dated correction that points to the earlier entry. Share the correction with the people who received the earlier report. Keep this template blank when sharing it as a public reference.
